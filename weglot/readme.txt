== Description ==

Weglot 2.0 is a website translation plugin that translates your WordPress site into 110+ languages, with AI translations built around your brand. Trusted by 110,000+ websites, Weglot detects and translates all of your content, from pages and posts to WooCommerce products, emails, and the text inside your images, then gives you full control to review and publish every translation. Weglot installs in 5 minutes, with no coding required.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==


= How do I translate WordPress with Weglot? =

To translate WordPress with Weglot, install the plugin, add your API key, and select your languages. Weglot detects all of your content, including pages, posts, and WooCommerce products, and translates it with its AI Translation Model. You then review each translation in context and publish when you are happy with it.

= What is Weglot 2.0? =

Weglot 2.0 builds on the Weglot you know. It brings a new AI Translation Model that translates around your brand, a redesigned translation experience with a new dashboard, Visual Editor and Translation List, Image Translation, and built-in multilingual visibility for SEO and GEO.

= Why does the plugin version not say 2.0? =

Weglot 2.0 is the name of the product generation, not the plugin release number. The plugin keeps its own version numbering, which is what you see in your WordPress dashboard when an update is available.

= What is a translation plugin for WordPress? =

A translation plugin for WordPress creates and serves translated versions of your content without duplicating your site. Weglot detects your content, translates it with AI built around your brand, and gives you a Visual Editor to review and refine every translation before it goes live.

= How to translate WordPress automatically? =

To translate WordPress automatically, Weglot scans and translates your content as soon as you publish it. Your translations stay synchronized across all languages, and you keep full control to review and edit any translation in the Visual Editor.

= Can I translate WordPress with AI? =

Yes, you can translate WordPress with AI using Weglot's AI Translation Model. It combines leading AI models with your Brand Profile, Translation Guidelines, and page context to produce translations that match the way your brand speaks in every language.

= Can Weglot translate the text inside my images? =

Yes. With Image Translation, you translate the text inside an image in one click from the Visual Editor, and Weglot preserves the original design. You can generate the translated image with AI or import your own version.

= Does Weglot translate WooCommerce? =

Yes, Weglot translates WooCommerce stores completely, from the product catalog to the checkout experience. It gives shoppers a localized buyer journey, which is critical for increasing international conversion rates.

= How do I translate WooCommerce products? =

To translate WooCommerce products, activate Weglot and it automatically detects your product titles, descriptions, and attributes. Use your Translation Guidelines to keep product names or brand terms in the original language, so your catalogue stays consistent across markets.

= Can Weglot translate WooCommerce emails? =

Yes, you can translate WooCommerce emails with Weglot so your customers receive order confirmations and shipping updates in their own language, for a professional post-purchase experience.

= How to make WordPress multilingual? =

To make WordPress multilingual, Weglot creates dedicated subdirectories (like /es/ or /fr/) for each language. This structure is recommended by Google and makes sure every translated page is indexed separately.

= Does Weglot support multilingual SEO? =

Weglot supports multilingual SEO by automatically adding hreflang tags and translating your metadata. This helps search engines understand which version of your website to show users in different countries. The plugin also lets you choose the site structure you prefer, such as subdirectories or subdomains.

= Will my translated pages show up in AI search? =

Weglot builds the multilingual SEO and GEO foundations that AI-powered answer engines rely on: language-specific URLs, hreflang tags, translated metadata, and pages ready for indexing. Your translated pages are ready to be discovered across search engines and AI-powered discovery experiences.

= Can I translate WordPress URLs? =

Yes, you can translate WordPress URLs with Weglot. Localizing your slugs (for example /shop/ to /boutique/) helps your search rankings and makes your website feel more authentic to local audiences.

= Does Weglot work with Elementor, Divi, and other page builders? =

Yes, Weglot works with page builders such as Elementor, Divi, Gutenberg, and Beaver Builder. Because the plugin translates content at the source code level, it detects and translates text inside any design element without breaking your layout or requiring a custom integration.

= Is Weglot compatible with other SEO plugins? =

Weglot is compatible with SEO plugins such as Yoast SEO, Rank Math, and All in One SEO. Weglot handles the technical side of multilingual SEO, like hreflang tags and translated metadata, and works alongside the SEO tools you already use.

= How does Weglot handle new content updates? =

Weglot scans your website for new posts and changes. Once detected, the new content is translated with your AI Translation Model and added to your Translation List, so you can review it and publish it in every language.

= Can I use a custom language switcher design? =

Yes, you can use a custom language switcher design with Weglot. We provide several built-in styles, including flags and dropdowns, and you can use custom CSS to match your branding. Place the switcher in your menu, as a widget, or anywhere on the page using a shortcode.

= Is my translation data secure and private? =

Your translation data is secure and private with Weglot. We are fully GDPR compliant and use encrypted connections to process your content. Your WordPress database stays untouched, and you keep full ownership of all your translations, which you can export at any time.
